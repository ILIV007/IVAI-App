# References and Clean-room Boundary

## Sources of truth

The repository documents and accepted decisions are the source of truth for implementation. The product roadmap and project office are planning sources; a task packet converts an accepted slice of that plan into an implementable change.

## Reference-only material

The following types of material may be reviewed for behavioral research, API patterns, UX observations, or compatibility analysis only:

- G0DM0D archives and documentation.
- Operit, PocketPal-AI, 9router, or other third-party source archives.
- Visual or motion references.
- Provider documentation and official Android documentation.

Reference material must not be copied into IVAI unless its license, attribution obligations, and fit with IVAI's Apache-2.0 policy have been explicitly reviewed. Incompatible copyleft code, uncensored-system prompts, telemetry/dataset designs, browser-local credential storage patterns, and server-only components are not transfer sources.

## Attribution workflow

Before adding any library or asset, record its name, version, source URL, license, reason for use, and whether a NOTICE obligation exists. Release preparation must generate and review dependency notices.
